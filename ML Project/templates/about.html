About Cancer Risk Prediction

Project Overview

This Cancer Risk Prediction system uses advanced machine learning to assess an individual's probability of having increased cancer risk based on various lifestyle, genetic, and demographic factors.

How It Works
📊
Data Collection

Collects relevant risk factors including age, lifestyle, family history, and medical background.

🤖
AI Analysis

Uses Random Forest algorithm trained on medical research data to analyze risk patterns.

📋
Risk Assessment

Provides personalized risk assessment with detailed explanations and recommendations.

Risk Factors Considered
  • Age: Cancer risk increases with age
  • BMI: Higher BMI can increase certain cancer risks
  • Smoking History: Major risk factor for many cancers
  • Alcohol Consumption: Linked to several cancer types
  • Physical Activity: Regular exercise reduces risk
  • Family History: Genetic predisposition to cancer
  • Genetic Markers: Inherited genetic mutations
  • Previous Cancer: History of cancer increases risk
  • Chronic Diseases: Underlying health conditions
